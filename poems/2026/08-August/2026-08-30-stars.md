---
title: Stars
poet: Sara Teasdale
date: 2026-08-30
---

Alone in the night
::line |~1em| |<On a dark hill|
With pines around me
::line |~1em| |<Spicy and still,|

And a heaven full of stars
::line |~1em| |<Over my head,|
White and topaz
::line |~1em| |<And misty red;|

Myriads with beating
::line |~1em| |<Hearts of fire|
That aeons
::line |~1em| |<Cannot vex or tire;|

Up the dome of heaven
::line |~1em| |<Like a great hill,|
I watch them marching
::line |~1em| |<Stately and still,|

And I know that I
::line |~1em| |<Am honored to be|
Witness
::line |~1em| |<Of so much majesty.|
