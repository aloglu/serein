---
title: Paper Flowers
poet: Jessica Jocelyn
date: 2026-08-12
---

Please, mother, don’t give birth to me.
Let me stay a wish in your heart,
your greatest *what if*.

Keep me folded in between the pages
of your imagined life,
untouched by the weight of your sadness.

Let me stay in the make-believe.
Where even paper flowers
think they are real.

Where the cycle ends silently
because I stay just a dream.
