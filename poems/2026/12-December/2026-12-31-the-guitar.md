---
title: The Guitar
poet: Federico García Lorca
translator: Cola Franzen
date: 2026-12-31
---

The weeping of the guitar
   begins.
   The goblets of dawn
   are smashed.
   The weeping of the guitar
   begins.
   Useless
   to silence it.
   Impossible
   to silence it.
   It weeps monotonously
   as water weeps
   as the wind weeps
   over snowfields.
   Impossible
   to silence it.
   It weeps for distant
   things.
   Hot southern sands
   yearning for white camellias.
   Weeps arrow without target
   evening without morning
   and the first dead bird
   on the branch.
   Oh, guitar!
   Heart mortally wounded
   by five swords.
