---
title: Coromandel Fishers
poet: Sarojini Naidu
date: 2026-12-12
---

Rise, brothers, rise, the wakening skies pray
::line |~2.5em| |<to the morning light,|
The wind lies asleep in the arms of the dawn
::line |~2.5em| |<like a child that has cried all night.|
Come, let us gather our nets from the shore,
::line |~2.5em| |<and set our catamarans free,|
To capture the leaping wealth of the tide, for
::line |~2.5em| |<we are the sons of the sea.|

No longer delay, let us hasten away in the
::line |~2.5em| |<track of the sea-gull’s call,|
The sea is our mother, the cloud is our brother,
::line |~2.5em| |<the waves are our comrades all.|
What though we toss at the fall of the sun
::line |~2.5em| |<where the hand of the sea-god drives?|
He who holds the storm by the hair, will hide
::line |~2.5em| |<in his breast our lives.|

Sweet is the shade of the cocoanut glade, and
::line |~2.5em| |<the scent of the mango grove,|
And sweet are the sands at the full o’ the
::line |~2.5em| |<moon with the sound of the voices we love.|
But sweeter, O brothers, the kiss of the spray
::line |~2.5em| |<and the dance of the wild foam’s glee:|
Row, brothers, row to the blue of the verge,
::line |~2.5em| |<where the low sky mates with the sea.|
