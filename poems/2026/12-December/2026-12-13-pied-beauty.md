---
title: Pied Beauty
poet: Gerard Manley Hopkins
date: 2026-12-13
---

GLORY be to God for dappled things—
::line |~1.5em| |<For skies of couple-colour as a brinded cow;|
::line |~3em| |<For rose-moles all in stipple upon trout that swim:|
Fresh-firecoal chestnut-falls; finches’ wings;
::line |~1.5em| |<Landscape plotted and pieced—fold, fallow, and|
::line |~3em| |<plough;|
::line |~2.5em| |<And àll tràdes, their gear and tackle and trim.|

All things counter, original, spare, strange;
::line |~1.5em| |<Whatever is fickle, freckled (who knows how?)|
::line |~3em| |<With swift, slow; sweet, sour; adazzle, dim;|
He fathers-forth whose beauty is past change:
::line |~9em| |<Praise him.|
