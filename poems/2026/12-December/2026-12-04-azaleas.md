---
title: Azaleas
poet: Kim Sowol
translator: David McCann
date: 2026-12-04
---

When you grow sick of me
And turn to leave,
Without a word, in peace, I’ll let you go.

On Yŏngbyŏn’s steep slopes
azaleas bloom —
An armful on your way I’ll pick and strew.

As step by step you walk
Upon the blooms,
Do lightly tread on them to go your way.

When you grow sick of me
And turn to leave,
Be it my death, please know I’ll shed no tears.
