---
title: Incident
poet: Countee Cullen
date: 2027-01-01
---

Once riding in old Baltimore,
::line |~1em| |<Heart-filled, head-filled with glee,|
I saw a Baltimorean
::line |~1em| |<Keep looking straight at me.|

Now I was eight and very small,
::line |~1em| |<And he was no whit bigger,|
And so I smiled, but he poked out
::line |~1em| |<His tongue and called me, “Nigger.”|

I saw the whole of Baltimore
::line |~1em| |<From May until December:|
Of all the things that happened there
::line |~1em| |<That’s all that I remember.|
