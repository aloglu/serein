---
title: Correspondences
poet: Charles Baudelaire
translator: F. P. Sturm
date: 2027-01-04
---

In Nature’s temple living pillars rise,
::line |~1em| |<And words are murmured none have understood.|
::line |~1em| |<And man must wander through a tangled wood|
Of symbols watching him with friendly eyes.

As long-drawn echoes heard far-off and dim
::line |~1em| |<Mingle to one deep sound and fade away;|
::line |~1em| |<Vast as the night and brilliant as the day,|
Colour and sound and perfume speak to him.

Some perfumes are as fragrant as a child,
::line |~1em| |<Sweet as the sound of hautboys, meadow-green;|
Others, corrupted, rich, exultant, wild,

Have all the expansion of things infinite:
::line |~1em| |<As amber, incense, musk, and benzoin,|
Which sing the sense’s and the soul’s delight.
